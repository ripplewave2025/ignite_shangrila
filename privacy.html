Draft for owner and legal review

Privacy should feel as respectful as the welcome.

This is a plain-language prototype, not a final legal notice. It must be reviewed against the venue’s real systems and current Indian requirements before launch.

What the proposed system remembers

Only information needed to manage a reservation or information a guest chooses to share: preferred name, contact channel, visit details, event interests, music preference, preferred area and celebration dates.

Separate choices

A reservation contact is used to manage that booking. Event emails, WhatsApp updates and personalization each require their own optional, unticked choice. Declining marketing never blocks a reservation.

What does not belong in a guest profile

No card or UPI details, ID copies, companions’ numbers, private gossip, scraped social profiles or sensitive personal labels. Door-level age checks should remain separate from marketing records.

Who can see it

Access should be limited to named managers and the host responsible for service. DJs and outside promoters receive only the event information they need—not guest spending or personal profiles.

Control belongs to the guest

The live site should offer a simple way to update preferences, unsubscribe, withdraw permission or request deletion. Opt-outs must immediately suppress future promotional messages.

Contact

The venue must nominate a privacy contact, retention period and data system before this page can become final.
